01 · TELL ME ABOUT YOURSELF

Homa. Rivrs. Now the intersection.

I have 5+ years in growth and user acquisition. At Homa, I managed large paid budgets and learned to think in ROAS, LTV, attribution and scale. At Rivrs, I now lead a team of seven across ten games, with a strong focus on creative systems, creators and international execution.

Today, I can combine paid rigor, creative velocity and the ability to build a growth function—not just operate one.

02 · WHY ARE YOU INTERESTED IN THIS ROLE?

I want to build, not inherit.

The role brings together the parts of growth I enjoy most: hands-on paid acquisition, structured experimentation, product measurement and close creative collaboration. I’m especially motivated when the playbook is still being written and I can help build it with the founders.

03 · WHAT WOULD YOU DO IN THE FIRST 90 DAYS?

Understand. Test. Scale.

01 · UNDERSTANDUsers, funnel, tracking and existing learnings.
02 · TESTAudience × message × creative, with clear hypotheses.
03 · SCALEQualified winners, gradually and with enough creative volume.

My first goal would not be to spend fast. It would be to build a reliable learning loop that tells us which users and messages create real product value.

04 · WHAT WOULD YOU TEST FIRST?

Start with the user, not the channel.

  • Audience: who has the strongest use case?
  • Message: which pain, promise or outcome matters?
  • Creative: how do we demonstrate the product clearly?

I would optimize for activated users—not just cheap clicks or signups.

05 · HOW DO YOU STRUCTURE AN EXPERIMENT?

One question. One variable. One success metric.

Before launch, I write down the hypothesis, the main variable and what result would change my decision. After the test, I document the learning and feed it into the next brief. The goal is not only to find a winner—it is to make the next test smarter.

Hypothesis→Test→Measure→Learn→Scale
06 · HOW WOULD YOU MEASURE PAID?

Connect the ad to product value.

Impression→Click→Signup→Activation→Retention→Revenue

My first activation hypothesis would be the user completing a meaningful first creation. But I would validate that against retention and revenue data. Attribution tells us where users came from; product data tells us whether they mattered.

07 · WHEN DO YOU SCALE?

When quality holds as spend grows.

I scale progressively once the audience, message and downstream quality are convincing. I watch marginal efficiency, activation and retention—not only platform ROAS. Budget and creative volume need to grow together, otherwise performance usually decays.

08 · HOW DO YOU WORK WITH CREATORS?

Give direction without killing the voice.

I give creators a clear angle, objective and proof point, but I avoid over-scripting. Then I test the creator, hook and execution separately and feed the learning into the next briefs.

At Rivrs: 50+ active creators · 20+ content accounts · 50+ videos per week · 5 languages.

09 · HOW DOES GAMING EXPERIENCE TRANSFER TO SOFTWARE?

The category changes. The learning system transfers.

I would not pretend the funnels are identical. But the core acquisition problem is transferable: understand user motivation, create messages that earn attention, connect acquisition to activation and retention, and scale only when the economics hold.

My e-commerce work with Noka also taught me direct-response creative and conversion outside gaming.

10 · ARE YOU STILL HANDS-ON?

Yes. Strategy stays close to execution.

I lead a team today, but I still work directly on channel strategy, creative concepts, analytics and experiment design. I like management when it removes friction and raises the quality of execution—not when it creates distance from the work.

11 · TELL ME ABOUT A FAILURE OR LEARNING

Cheap traffic can be expensive learning.

I have seen campaigns generate attractive top-of-funnel numbers while bringing low-quality users downstream. The lesson was simple: define the business metric before launching, and make the signal visible quickly enough to stop or adapt the test.

12 · HOW WOULD YOU REPORT TO FOUNDERS?

Decision first. Dashboard second.

  • Spend and qualified acquisition cost.
  • Activation, retention and revenue signal.
  • What we learned and what we test next.

Founders should not have to read Ads Manager to know what decision to make.

13 · WHY DO YOU WANT TO MOVE?

I want to own the full learning loop.

I want to focus my experience on one ambitious consumer product and build paid acquisition from experimentation through scale. The most attractive part is being close to product, creative and the founders, with enough ownership to turn learnings into action quickly.

QUESTIONS FOR THEM

Use the conversation to diagnose the opportunity.

Pick four to six depending on the conversation. Start with the business, then go deeper on measurement, scope and team.

01 · PRODUCT & GROWTH

Where is the real constraint?

  • What is the biggest growth constraint today: awareness, acquisition, activation, retention or monetization?
  • Which users retain best, and what use case or behavior makes them different?
  • What product event currently predicts long-term value most reliably?
  • Which product or onboarding changes could unlock paid acquisition over the next six months?
02 · PAID & ECONOMICS

What would make paid scalable?

  • What have you already tested in paid, and where did performance break down?
  • Which metric should ultimately govern scaling: activated CAC, payback period, retention or contribution margin?
  • Do you already have an LTV model or a payback target you trust?
  • Is the immediate priority to prove one repeatable channel or to build a broader acquisition mix?
03 · MEASUREMENT & CREATIVE

Can we learn fast enough?

  • How well can ad exposure connect to signup, activation, retention and revenue today?
  • What is the current time lag between launching a test and getting a reliable quality signal?
  • How is creative produced today, and what is the realistic weekly testing capacity?
  • How do you imagine creators and UGC working with the paid acquisition loop?
04 · SCOPE & TEAM

What am I being asked to build?

  • How many people are in the growth team today, and who would I work with day to day?
  • Would I have direct reports from the start, or begin as an individual contributor?
  • If the channel proves scalable, would I have the mandate and budget to recruit my own team?
  • Which decisions would I fully own, and which would stay shared with founders, product or creative?
05 · EXPECTATIONS

What does success look like?

  • What would make you say after six months that hiring me was a great decision?
  • What should be demonstrably true after the first 30, 60 and 90 days?
  • Where do you want the growth function to be twelve months from now?
  • What is the hardest unresolved question you expect this hire to answer?
06 · WORKING TOGETHER

How will we make decisions?

  • How do founders, product and growth currently decide what gets prioritized?
  • How comfortable is the team with stopping a promising idea when the data disagrees?
  • What level of speed and autonomy do you expect from this role?
  • What is one thing about the challenge that is difficult to see from outside the company?
